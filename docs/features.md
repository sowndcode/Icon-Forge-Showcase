# Features

Everything Icon Forge Showcase can do, in the order you would use it. The [README](../README.md) has
the short version.

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| Templates | 77, in 9 groups |
| Layouts | 35 |
| Themes | 59 built-in, plus themes made from your icons' colours |
| Canvas presets | 60 (itch.io, Steam, asset stores, social media, video) |
| Export | PNG, WebP or JPEG at 1×, 2×, 4× or a custom scale |
| Platform | Windows 10/11, fully offline |

## 1. Import your icons

- **Files or whole folders:** PNG, WebP, JPEG, AVIF, GIF and BMP.
- **Categories for free:** subfolders become categories, and auto-categorize suggests more from file
  names.
- **Visible-pixel analysis:** each image's transparent padding is measured, so layouts, alignment and
  snapping use what you can actually see.
- **Asset library:** large preview with zoom and file details, an adjustable grid, filters by format,
  transparency and size, and duplicate detection by file content.

## 2. Generate a whole store set

From Home, **Complete store set** builds, in one step and one matching look:

- a hero image (1920 × 1080),
- a square cover (1080 × 1080),
- a numbered catalog of every icon (3000 × 3000),
- one page per category.

**Custom store set** lets you choose which of those to make; **Catalog** and **Category pages** make
only those. Every generated page stays fully editable.

## 3. Start from a template

77 templates that fill themselves with your icons:

| Group | For |
|---|---|
| itch.io covers | 630 × 500 store covers |
| Clean & professional | Restrained store pages, catalogs and social posts with real facts about the pack |
| Game UI mockups | Your icons in an inventory with a tooltip card, on a hotbar, or at exact pixel sizes |
| Wide thumbnails | 1920 × 1080 gallery and screenshot images |
| Square & social | Square posts, stories and portrait promos |
| Banners & headers | Steam capsules, YouTube thumbnails, launch banners |
| Free demos | A free sample of the pack with a FREE DEMO badge |
| Sales & comparisons | Colour vs grayscale, free vs full |
| Catalogs | Every icon, organised, up to 4K |

- **Facts come from your files:** icon counts, pixel sizes, formats, names and categories are read
  from the pack, never invented.
- **Smart recommendations** (Settings → General, on by default) list the templates that suit your
  pack first, with a short reason. None is ever hidden.
- **Live options:** each template page keeps its own format, look, text style and icon size; changing
  one changes only that.
- **Apply part of a template:** right-click to apply only its layout, background or text style to the
  pages you have selected.

## 4. Arrange with layouts

35 layouts that space icons by their visible pixels:

- **Organise:** grid, packed grid, masonry, rows, columns, cascade, category blocks, feature grid.
- **Shapes:** circle, ring, arc, spiral, fan, orbit, pyramid, honeycomb, shape fill, diagonal flow,
  custom path.
- **Showcase:** scatter, cluster, round cluster, dual cluster, side bands, edge frame, split, hero
  composition, hero showcase, compare.
- **Perspective:** endless floor, tilted wall, cropped frame, perspective floor and wall, and a ground
  plane with icons lying flat.

Random layouts are seeded so you can get the same result back, and icons you move by hand are pinned.
The layout picker has search, favourites, recently used and live previews of your own icons.

## 5. Edit anything

- **Canvas:** move, scale, rotate, flip, group and lock, with smart guides while you move and resize;
  a safe zone and a focal region that layouts keep clear.
- **Text and badges:** titles, badges, labels and pixel-style text with gradient fill, outlines, 3D
  depth, glow, shadow and curved text. Type `{count}` to show the current number of icons.
- **Simple and Advanced text modes:** Simple offers coordinated looks that read on the actual
  background, with a contrast check and one-click fixes; Advanced has every control.
- **Image effects:** opacity, grayscale, glow, outline, and shapes that restyle what lies under them.
- **60 canvas presets** for itch.io, Steam, asset stores, social media and video, or any custom size.

## 6. Choose a look

- **59 themes:** genre themes, 15 restrained professional themes and 10 palettes (muted, pastel,
  industrial and deep premium).
- **Themes from your icons:** clean light, dark, soft, vivid, warm and cool variants built from your
  icons' colours, with readable contrast.
- **Backgrounds:** solids, gradients, mesh blends, procedural patterns and spotlight glows, including
  backgrounds matched to your assets.
- Search, favourites, recently used, a before/after preview, and apply to several pages at once.

## 7. Review and protect

- **Review** each page at Fit, pixel-accurate 100% or thumbnail size, with platform crops and
  store-card mock-ups.
- **Composition warnings** for text that is too small, too close to the edge or outside the safe zone,
  each with a one-click fix.
- **Watermark:** text and logo layers (single, repeated or on each icon) shown in Review and exports,
  never baked into your pages, with presets and smart placement. Compare clean, watermarked or side by
  side.

## 8. Export

- PNG, WebP or JPEG at 1×, 2×, 4× or a custom scale, each page rendered fresh from your original files.
- **Batch export** with a naming pattern: `{project}`, `{page}`, `{index}`, `{w}`, `{h}`, `{date}`,
  `{time}`. Existing files are never overwritten.
- Preview the exact output file, save export presets, and save a clean and a watermarked copy of each
  page in one go.

## 9. Projects, libraries and sharing

- **Projects** autosave, keep earlier versions and can be recovered from autosaves. Deleted projects
  go to a Trash you can restore from. The project browser has search, pinning and multi-select.
- **Portable `.showcase` files** hold a project with its original images and fonts. A new project can
  start empty or reuse chosen assets from another project.
- **Local libraries:** your themes, backgrounds, templates and elements are available in every
  project, and you can import them from another project's pages.
- **My themes and share codes:** save a page's look as your own theme, and share themes and templates
  as a short text code. Codes work offline, contain no images or personal data, and are checked and
  previewed before anything is added.

## 10. Learn as you go

- **Guide:** twelve interactive tutorials that point at the real controls, work offline and remember
  where you stopped.
