**Icon Forge Showcase** turns a folder of transparent game assets (icons, items, sprites) into
marketplace images: itch.io covers, wide thumbnails, social posts, banners, free-demo covers and full
catalogs. It's a desktop app for Windows that runs entirely on your computer: no account, no uploads, no
telemetry, and it never connects to the internet.

> **This is an early alpha.** It's usable day to day, but expect rough edges, and features, menus and
> the look of the app will still change. Keep backups of important work (Ctrl+S saves a portable
> `.showcase` file).

## Highlights

- **Import** files or whole folders (PNG, WebP, JPEG, AVIF, GIF, BMP). Subfolders become categories, and
  every layout uses each image's visible bounds, not its transparent padding.
- **35 smart layouts:** grids, honeycomb, masonry, circles, spirals, scatter, clusters and perspective
  floors and walls. Random layouts are seeded, and icons you move by hand stay put.
- **68 built-in templates** for itch.io covers, wide thumbnails, square and social posts, banners, free
  demos, sales and catalogs, with **51 themes** plus themes made from your own icons' colours.
- **Text and badges** with outlines, 3D depth, glow, curved text, a `{count}` token and a readability check.
- **Review** each page at Fit, 100% or thumbnail size, with platform crops and store-card mock-ups.
- **Export** PNG, WebP or JPEG at 1×, 2×, 4× or a custom scale, rendered fresh from your original files,
  with batch naming patterns and an optional watermark. Existing files are never overwritten.
- **Projects** autosave, keep earlier versions, go to a Trash before they're deleted, and can be saved as
  portable `.showcase` files.
- **Guide:** twelve interactive tutorials that point at the real controls.

## Install (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

1. Download **`IconForgeShowcase-0.2.0-alpha.1-win-x64.zip`** from **Assets** below.
2. Right-click the ZIP ▸ **Extract All…** and extract it to a folder you can write to, for example
   `Documents\Icon Forge Showcase`. Don't run it from inside the ZIP, and avoid `C:\Program Files`: your
   projects are saved in a `data` folder next to the app.
3. Double-click **`IconForgeShowcase.exe`**.
4. The app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show *"Windows protected your PC"*. Choose
   **More info ▸ Run anyway**.

No installer, Node.js or internet connection is needed. To remove the app, delete its folder (back up or
export your projects first: they're in its `data` folder).

## Known limitations

- **Windows only.** Other systems are untested.
- **Unsigned builds** (SmartScreen warning) and **no auto-update**: to update, download the new ZIP and
  copy its files over the old folder, or extract it elsewhere and let it copy your projects
  ([details](https://github.com/sowndcode/Icon-Forge-Showcase/blob/main/RELEASING.md#updating-an-installed-copy)).
- **The project format may still change** during the alpha. Older projects are migrated automatically,
  but keep `.showcase` backups of important work.
- **Fonts:** templates use fonts that come with Windows (Segoe UI, Bahnschrift, Impact). Custom fonts can
  be imported per project.
- **The app folder must be writable.** If it isn't, the app warns you and saves to your user profile
  for that session.
- Text placed directly over icons isn't checked for readability (backgrounds and panels are).

The full list of changes is in the [changelog](https://github.com/sowndcode/Icon-Forge-Showcase/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
